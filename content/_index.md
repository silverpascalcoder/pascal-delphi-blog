## Modern Pascal

Modern Pascal is a practical look at the entire Pascal ecosystem — not just the classic language, but the full family of modern implementations including **Delphi**, **FreePascal**, **Object Pascal**, and Wirth‑inspired descendants. If it compiles Pascal, extends Pascal, or keeps the lineage alive, it belongs here.

This site focuses on clear explanations, real examples, and commentary that helps developers understand how Pascal works today — across compilers, platforms, and modern tooling.

## What “Pascal” Means Here

On this site, the term **Pascal** always refers to the broader family:

- Standard Pascal  
- Object Pascal  
- Delphi  
- FreePascal  
- Modern Pascal variants  
- Wirth‑lineage descendants and compatible dialects  

Unless stated otherwise, any reference to *Pascal* includes **Delphi** and all major modern implementations.
