# About Modern Pascal

Modern Pascal exists to explore the full Pascal family — Delphi, FreePascal, Object Pascal, and Wirth‑style languages — with clear, practical commentary. The goal is simple: explain how these languages work today, why they matter, and how they fit into modern programming practice.

I’ve worked with Pascal and relate languages for decades, formal methods, and real‑world development. My background includes computing, formal specification, and experience with FreePascal, Delphi, and modern Pascal variants.

This site focuses on clarity, correctness, and practical insight. No nostalgia, no hype — just grounded explanations that help developers understand the ecosystem as it actually is.

If you’re interested in the Pascal family — historically or in modern use — you’re in the right place.
